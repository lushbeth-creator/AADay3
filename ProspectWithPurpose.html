Worth the next conversation?

  • There is a real operational or payment need.
  • The opportunity fits Propelr solutions and risk appetite.
  • The right people can participate.
  • There is a credible next step and timeline.

Warm pathways

Work your sphere of influence and referral relationships alongside cold outreach.

Software clue

Ask what software runs the business. A shared platform may signal an ISV opportunity that needs the proper internal handoff.

Coach the choice

Ask: "What evidence tells you this merchant is worth the next conversation?" Look for a specific business need, access to the right person and a next step that can be scheduled.
