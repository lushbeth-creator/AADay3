Follow the merchant journey from the first conversation through the decision, launch and long-term relationship.

Earn the Conversation

Start with relevance.

Prospect with purpose, deliver a relevant pitch and secure dedicated time. The goal is not to explain everything at the door. Earn enough trust and curiosity to continue the conversation.

Discover and Recommend

Let discovery lead.

Uncover the business need, read the evidence and connect it to the right solution. Ask, listen and use the merchant's facts to build a recommendation that solves a meaningful business problem.

Protect the Relationship

Stay connected after the yes.

Set honest expectations, make a clean handoff and remain useful after launch. The Aviator protects trust through accurate expectations, a strong handoff and thoughtful follow-up while Partner Development guides Stratus and onboarding.
