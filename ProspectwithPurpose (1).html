A strong pipeline starts with focus. Business type, operating model, payment environment and access to the decision-maker all affect fit and sales-cycle length.

Business profile

Identify the vertical, locations, ownership structure and whether the business is an SMB, franchise or larger operation.

Payment environment

Notice checkout friction, aging equipment, reporting gaps, long lines or unresolved service issues.

Decision access

Learn who influences the decision, who approves it and when those people are available.

Solution fit

Confirm that the opportunity aligns with Propelr solutions, current underwriting guidance and the merchant's operating needs.
