Recommend, verify and ask.

The proposal should feel like the logical result of discovery. Translate every feature into an operational advantage and a merchant outcome. Competing on rate alone makes the relationship easy to replace.

Feature to outcome
Reliable equipment→Less checkout disruption and fewer missed sales
Clear reporting→Faster reconciliation and better visibility
Responsive support→Less downtime and a clear escalation path
Right-fit solution→A payment experience aligned to how the merchant operates
Statement lens

Net effective rate

Use NER to orient the conversation, then examine card mix, pricing structure and fees before drawing conclusions.

Estimated NER0.00%Total fees ÷ total volume. A diagnostic, not a stand-alone competitiveness verdict.

Confirm readiness

"If we can resolve the priorities we identified, are you comfortable moving forward?"

Prepare the file

Statements, voided check, tax ID, identification and any underwriting documents required for the business type.

Explain what follows

Set honest expectations for review, approval, equipment, configuration, training and go-live.

Field guide

Handle the moment without losing ground.

Acknowledge: the concern.
Clarify: ask what they are actually comparing.
Next move: return to total value and review the complete statement. Do not promise savings until the analysis supports it.
Coach the recommendation

Ask: "Which merchant priority does each part of your recommendation solve?" If an element cannot be tied to a need uncovered in discovery, it may not belong in the presentation.
