Build a pitch that opens a door.

Your opening should sound human, not rehearsed. The goal is not to explain payments on the spot. It is to create enough relevance and trust to schedule a discovery conversation.

Coach the opening

Ask: "Where did the merchant become more engaged?" Then isolate the behavior that created it. Was it relevance, brevity, curiosity or the way the agent respected the merchant's time?
