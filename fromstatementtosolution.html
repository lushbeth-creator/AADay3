Statement lens

Net effective rate

Use NER to orient the conversation, then examine card mix, pricing structure and fees before drawing conclusions.

How To Calculate It

NER = Total Fees ÷ Total Amount Submitted

Pull both numbers from the merchant's statement, divide fees by volume, then move the decimal two spots to the right to get the percentage.

Where Fees Hide
  • Junk fees — small, vaguely-named charges with no clear service behind them.
  • Batch fees — a charge every time the merchant closes out the day's transactions.
  • Header/PCI fees — recurring line items that can be marked up well above actual cost.
  • Anything marked up or duplicated across categories is worth flagging, this is often where the real savings live, not just the headline rate.
